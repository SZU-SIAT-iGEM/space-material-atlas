# Contributing

See the [contribution guide](docs/contributing.md) for content, layout and documentation changes, and the [maintenance guide](docs/maintenance.md) for running the local studio.

Open an issue or pull request in [space-material-atlas](https://github.com/SZU-SIAT-iGEM/space-material-atlas). For a content correction, identify the team/node, language, source and proposed wording. Preserve stable IDs and unrelated routes. Do not commit private drafts or credentials.

Contributions are accepted under the applicable [project licenses](docs/licensing.md). Retain attribution for material from other teams and submit only material you are entitled to share. Content remains a prerelease until reviewed.
